# PBI 501.002: Richer ask channel for headless runs

**Priority:** P3
**Dependencies:** PBI 002.003

## Description

Beyond comment-and-stop: a run that needs Sam reaches him where he is, the answer arrives without an orient, and the resumption is immediate. Candidates include a push notification to CV or the phone, and a run that waits briefly before checkpointing. Deferred until the frequency and cost of asks are observed in v1.

Traces to Architecture decision 10 and RESEARCH remaining unknowns.

## Deliverables

- An ask notifies Sam on a channel he chooses, in addition to the PR comment.
- An answer given on that channel resumes the run without a separate orient and save.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] An ask reaches Sam's chosen channel within a minute of the run stopping.
- [ ] Answering on that channel resumes the run.

## Testing Requirements

- [ ] An end-to-end ask and resume over the chosen channel.
